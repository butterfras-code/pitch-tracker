## Pattern prompt

Create a finished square raster texture asset for an offline music classroom app skin named Lisa Lives!, inspired by Lisa Frank 1990s maximalist rainbow school stationery. This is NOT a website or UI mockup. Edge-to-edge repeating pattern, vivid rainbow airbrushed diagonal bands of hot magenta, yellow, acid lime, turquoise, cobalt and violet, overlaid with bold organic black leopard rosettes and flowing zebra stripes. Scatter small crisp holographic five-point star stickers and tiny white sparkle glints. Saturated, joyful, candy colors, glossy iridescent foil, nostalgic sticker binder cover. Full bleed, no margin, no frame, no lettering, no logos, no interface, no central animal face. The pattern must remain recognizable cropped into narrow rims and strips around dark reading panels. Large clear animal markings, detailed high quality printing, bright maximum saturation. Output asset only.

## Sticker prompt

Create one polished die-cut sticker asset on a truly transparent background, square canvas. Lisa Frank inspired 1990s rainbow school stationery: adorable rainbow leopard cub HEAD ONLY, large sparkling turquoise eyes, pink nose, round ears, lush smoothly airbrushed cyan-magenta-violet-yellow-lime fur with black leopard spots. Happy friendly expression, looking at viewer. White and holographic rainbow foil outline around head silhouette. Two small holographic five-point star stickers tucked beside the ears. Saturated brilliant candy colors, glossy printed sticker look, crisp clean edges. Face fills canvas with modest transparent margin. No body, no words, no logo, no shadow outside sticker, no background scene, no UI.
