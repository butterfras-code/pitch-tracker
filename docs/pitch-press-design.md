# Pitch Press

Pitch Press is an inked broadside on coarse rag stock. Archival black surrounds
aged paper plates, reversed grading blocks, and a reversed selected student.
Color is reserved for live pitch feedback and the pressed grading state.

## Type and materials

Bundled Smokum (Press Wood), an ornamental condensed display face by Astigmatic,
sets body copy, buttons, navigation, metadata labels, and every student name.
Alfa Slab One (Press Slab) remains the concert-note face. Hz and cents keep a
tabular monospace face so measured data is easy to compare.

Small ink-squeeze shadows and alternating half-degree name impressions suggest
uneven type placement. These are fixed impressions, not continuous jitter.
Controls retain their shared geometry and stable hit areas.

The generated press-rag-paper.webp supplies visible cotton fibers, flecks, foxing,
and water marks. Paper surfaces receive a light translucent stock wash; black
plates multiply the same texture into their ink. Roster cards vary the texture
origin to avoid identical stains. Double rules carry a distressed, slightly
misregistered ink impression. Texture masks apply only to decorative borders,
never whole controls, text, or focus rings.

## Print-shop furniture

Crop marks sit in the viewport margin. A tiny registration slug reads
“PLATE 04-B // MOUTHPIECE PRESS,” with crosshair marks. The plate identifier is
decorative, not a session ID or job date. Generated content has an empty accessible
alternative, intercepts no pointer events, and disappears on theme changes and
physical printing.

## Controls and feedback

Grading controls are black ink blocks with reversed paper lettering. Active low,
in-range, and high readings switch to brick, forest, and Prussian blue ink; the
functional meter blade follows the live range color. A brief press strike marks
activation. Historical results use neutral ink and do not replay the strike.

Pressed controls move down three pixels and compress their shadow. Reduced motion
suppresses animation and transitions. Keyboard focus remains visible in either
paper or ink surroundings.

## Boundaries and validation

All decoration is scoped to Pitch Press. The fonts, grain, and ink mask are local
assets inlined into the single-file build. Shared layout, audio, scoring,
persistence, and navigation remain unchanged.

Offline Chromium/Firefox checks cover phone, fullscreen laptop and desktop Split,
roster scrolling, loaded typography, reversed selection, neutral/active grading
inks, reduced motion, focus, playback, recording, and clean theme reset.
The material prompt and font licensing are recorded in docs/licenses.
